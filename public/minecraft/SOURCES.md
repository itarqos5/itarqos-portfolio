# Minecraft assets

Acquired through the integrated browser from [Scenes from the Overworld](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/scenes-overworld), by Mojang Studios, on 2026-09-08. These are authentic Minecraft scenes, not generated images or screenshots of the portfolio projects.

- swamp.avif: SoothingScene_rain.jpg
- beach.webp: SoothingScene_beach.jpg
- ocean.jpg: SoothingScene_ocean.jpg
- snow.avif: SoothingScene_snow.jpg
- cherry.jpg: SS_Cherry_dotNET_img_01_1170x500.jpg

Image source directory: https://www.minecraft.net/content/dam/minecraftnet/games/minecraft/key-art/

Minecraft Ten and Minecraft Seven fonts were obtained from the same rendered official page. Original URLs:
- https://www.minecraft.net/etc.clientlibs/minecraftnet/clientlibs/clientlib-site/resources/fonts/Minecraft-Tenv2.woff2
- https://www.minecraft.net/etc.clientlibs/minecraftnet/clientlibs/clientlib-site/resources/fonts/Minecraft-Seven_v2.woff2

Geist Latin is the existing portfolio's Google font, copied from its previously built assets so preview and production builds have no remote font dependency.

Hero display derivative: `swamp-4k-soft.webp` is a 3840px-wide Lanczos3 upscale of `swamp.avif` (864 x 505), with a 1.1px Gaussian blur baked in and WebP quality 94. This is conventional image resampling, not generated imagery or a native 4K source. Only the hero uses this derivative; the original is retained.

Ocean-theme update: `ocean-4k-soft.webp` is the same conventional 3840px Lanczos3 upscale and light blur treatment, sourced from `ocean.jpg`. No AI-generated assets are used.

The two playable films are official Minecraft videos linked by the source article:
- Soothing Underwater Scenes: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RnnctM5Rf9I
- Tranquil Beach: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SZu6k3riYso

Videos use click-to-load YouTube privacy-enhanced embeds. No video file was generated or downloaded. Thumbnails are the matching official scenery images, and the original YouTube links remain available.
