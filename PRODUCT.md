# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Potential software-development clients, employers evaluating technical ability, and open-source collaborators exploring Literal's work.

## Product Purpose

A personal developer portfolio that presents Literal's projects, server work, reviews, skills, and technology experience. Success means visitors understand the breadth and quality of the work and choose to contact Literal on Discord.

## Positioning

Literal builds performance-focused systems across Java, Netty, Rust, Minecraft infrastructure, databases, and modern web interfaces.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the portfolio, inspect featured work and technical experience, follow project links to GitHub, and can contact Literal through Discord.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current sections and their factual content.
- Section layouts and presentation may be redesigned completely.
- Discord contact is the primary action; GitHub and other existing external links remain available.
- The existing Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, and Framer Motion stack remains in place.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the name “Literal,” existing profile image, projects, reviews, skills, technologies, and links.
- The replacement visual identity is black and white, space-themed, modern, and motion-led.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the profile image at `public/profile.png`, project descriptions and repository links, server listings, client reviews, technology experience, and existing social/contact links. No additional commercial claims should be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Make technical credibility understandable to both technical and non-technical visitors.
- Let real projects and reviews carry the proof.
- Make Discord contact obvious without obscuring exploration.
- Preserve useful detail while keeping the page easy to scan.
- Use motion to deepen the experience without compromising access or performance.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, clear focus states, sufficient contrast, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion preferences.
